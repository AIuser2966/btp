Nifty moves almost identically (correlation 0.999); the funds trail by ~0.3–0.5%/yr (fund costs, and actual dividends a little below the 1.3% assumed). Gold ETFs follow international gold closely (they differ by import duty changes and ETF costs). Liquid funds earn 0.3–0.5%/yr more than the T-bill index, and their monthly wiggles differ (they are tiny), which is expected: funds also hold money-market paper, not only T-bills.
